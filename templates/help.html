🚜 Farmer Guide

How do I submit a transport request?

1. Navigate to the Farmer Dashboard from the main menu

2. Click "Submit New Request"

3. Fill in pickup location, destination, goods type, and quantity

4. Click "Request Transport" and confirm

5. Our AI will automatically match you with a nearby driver

How are drivers assigned to my request?

AgriMove AI uses smart logistics algorithms to match your request with the nearest available driver based on:

  • Driver proximity to pickup location
  • Driver availability status
  • Vehicle capacity for your goods
  • Driver rating and performance

Matching usually happens within 5 minutes of request submission.

What are delivery charges?

Pricing Structure:

  • Base fee: ₵5.00
  • Distance: ₵2.50 per kilometer
  • Peak hours surcharge: +20% (6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 5-7 PM)
  • Tax: +5% on total

You can see the exact quote before confirming your request.

How do I track my delivery?

1. Go to Farmer Dashboard → View Requests

2. Find your delivery and click "Track Delivery"

3. See real-time driver location and ETA

4. Get SMS notifications at each stage (Accepted, In Transit, Delivered)

🚗 Driver Guide

How do I accept delivery jobs?

1. Go to Driver Dashboard

2. View available requests in the "Open Requests" section

3. Review destination, goods type, and estimated earnings

4. Click "Accept Job" to accept the delivery

5. Click "Start Delivery" when ready to pick up

How do I toggle my availability?

1. In Driver Dashboard, click the availability toggle button

2. "Available" - You can receive new delivery requests

3. "Busy" - You cannot receive new requests (automatically set when you accept a job)

4. Toggle resets automatically when you complete a delivery

How are my earnings calculated?

Earnings = Base fare + Distance charge - Platform fee (2%)

Example:

  • Base: ₵5.00
  • Distance (20 km): ₵50.00
  • Subtotal: ₵55.00
  • Platform fee (2%): ₵1.10
  • Your earning: ₵53.90

How do ratings affect me?

Ratings are crucial for driver success on AgriMove AI:

  • 4.8+ rating: Unlock premium jobs and surge pricing
  • Below 4.0: May limit new delivery requests
  • Consistent delivery and professionalism improve ratings

You can improve ratings by:

  • Delivering on time
  • Taking care of goods
  • Being professional with farmers
  • Following navigation instructions

💳 Payment & Billing

What payment methods are accepted?

  • 💳 Visa / Mastercard
  • 📱 Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, Airteltigo)
  • 🏦 Bank Transfer
  • 💰 PayPal
  • 🤝 Cash on Delivery (COD) in select areas

When is payment due?

For Farmers: Payment is due immediately after delivery confirmation

For Drivers: Earnings are processed within 24 hours to your registered bank account or mobile wallet

Invoices and receipts are generated automatically for all transactions.

Can I get a refund?

Refund Policy:

  • Cancel before driver accepts: Full refund
  • Cancel after driver accepts: 50% refund (if within 5 minutes)
  • Cancel after pickup: No refund (delivery will proceed)
  • Non-delivery disputes: Reviewed within 24 hours

Contact support with invoice number and reason for refund request.

How do I access invoices?

1. Go to your Dashboard → Completed Deliveries

2. Click on any delivery to view details

3. Click "View Invoice" to see itemized charges

4. Use "Print" or "Download PDF" for records

All invoices are also emailed automatically after delivery completion.

🔧 Technical Support

The app is running slow or freezing

Try these steps:

  • Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Check internet connection speed
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily

If problem persists, contact support with your browser and device info.

I'm not receiving SMS notifications

Troubleshooting:

  • Verify phone number in your profile settings
  • Check spam/junk SMS folder
  • Ensure notifications are enabled in settings
  • Check if your phone number is in correct format (+233...)
  • Contact your telecom provider to check for SMS blocks

GPS tracking shows wrong location

For drivers:

  • Enable location services on your phone
  • Grant AgriMove permission to access location
  • Ensure GPS is turned on
  • Wait 2-3 seconds for GPS to acquire signal
  • Refresh the tracking page

Note: GPS accuracy varies by location and can have 5-50m margin of error.

I can't log in or reset password

Steps to resolve:

  • Click "Forgot Password" on login page
  • Enter your registered email
  • Check email for reset link (check spam folder)
  • Click link and create new password
  • Try logging in again

If still having issues, contact our support team.
