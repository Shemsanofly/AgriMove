💡 Profit Optimization Tips

🚚

Reduce Transport Costs

Bundle with other farmers to reduce per-unit transport. Every 10,000 TZS saved = potential 50,000 TZS more profit.

⏰

Timing is Key

Wait for price peaks (rising trend). Use the Market Prices tool to track trends and ship when prices are high.

📦

Minimize Losses

Use better packaging and faster transport. We estimate 5% loss - good logistics can reduce this to 2-3%.

🎯

Target High Demand

Ship to high-demand markets. High demand = higher prices + faster sales. Check demand levels in Market Prices.
