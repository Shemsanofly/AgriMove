💡 Tips for Maximizing Profits

🎯

Target High Demand Markets

Look for regions with "High" demand levels. These markets pay premium prices and have quick turnarounds.

📈

Follow Price Trends

Wait for "Rising" trends before harvesting. Time your deliveries when prices are trending upward.

🚚

Optimize Transport Costs

Use the Profit Estimator to calculate best routes. Lower transport = higher profit margin.

🤝

Pool Resources

Combine with other farmers to reach minimum volumes. Bulk shipments reduce per-unit transport costs.
