Frequently Asked Questions

When do I earn points?

Points are awarded automatically when a delivery is marked as "Delivered" on AgriMove AI. Both farmers and drivers earn points.

Do points expire?

No! Your points never expire. Keep accumulating them indefinitely.

Can I transfer points?

Points are tied to individual accounts and cannot be transferred.

What if I redeem points and change my mind?

Contact our support team within 24 hours for assistance with reversals.
